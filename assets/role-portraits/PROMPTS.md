# Portrait prompt set

These seven fictional portraits were created with the built-in image generation tool. Each image used this shared instruction:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square portrait for a fictional youth workshop role card. Style: cohesive modern editorial cartoon illustration, clean confident outlines, softly textured flat colors, warm human character, clearly non-photorealistic and not resembling any real person. Composition: head and shoulders centered, face fully visible, generous margin for cropping. No national flags, stereotypes, text, letters, logos, watermark, or extra people. Coordinated set of seven portraits with consistent framing and visual language.

Character details supplied for each image:

1. **Eydís Jónsdóttir:** Fictional 19-year-old youth arts organizer from Reykjavík, Iceland; friendly, curious expression; short wavy dark auburn hair; simple teal sweater; small playful paint-splatter pin; pale coral background.
2. **Jógvan Joensen:** Fictional 20-year-old man and community chat moderator from Tórshavn, Faroe Islands; warm but alert expression; short wavy dark brown hair; subtle stubble; round glasses; deep blue cardigan; tiny speech-bubble pin; pale peach background.
3. **Ingrid Solheim:** Fictional 18-year-old youth club welcomer from Trondheim, Norway; open, encouraging expression; medium brown skin; short natural curls; mustard yellow hoodie; tiny star pin; pale lavender background.
4. **Elias Karlsson:** Fictional 19-year-old man and volunteer organizer from Mariehamn, Åland; confident, thoughtful expression; warm olive skin; short dark blond hair with a neat side part; rust-orange jacket; tiny notebook pin; pale mint background.
5. **Linnea Sjöberg:** Fictional 20-year-old student writer from Umeå, Sweden; inquisitive, slightly playful expression; fair skin; shoulder-length copper blonde hair with a side part; forest green top; tiny pencil pin; pale sky-blue background.
6. **Mikko Lehtinen:** Fictional 21-year-old man and peer discussion facilitator from Turku, Finland; kind but serious expression; Finnish man with East Asian heritage; short straight black hair; plum-colored sweater; tiny conversation-circle pin; pale buttery-yellow background.
7. **Mats Berg:** Fictional 20-year-old newcomer to online communities from Tromsø, Norway; thoughtful, approachable expression; medium brown skin; close-cropped curly black hair; soft blue sweatshirt; tiny compass pin; pale sage-green background.

The Jógvan, Elias, and Mikko prompts also explicitly asked the image tool to depict young adult men. The first Eydís prompt used slightly more explicit wording (“portrait 1 of a coordinated set of seven”). All images are new fictional illustrations, not based on photos of real people.
