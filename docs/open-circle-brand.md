# Open Circle identity

The participant-facing forum is Open Circle. Its header pairs the generated mark with a live-text wordmark; the same mark is the favicon and appears on the welcome page. The conference and facilitation identity stays in the private planning material.

- Final asset: `dist/brand/open-circle-mark.png` (1254 × 1254 PNG with transparency).
- Generation method: built-in image-generation tool.
- Colors: burgundy `#682241` and pink `#EA528E`, matching the existing interface.
- User direction: make the forum feel like an established community, without Hacked or fictional/workshop labels in the normal participant view. The host-controlled incident and its eventual explanation retain their existing behavior.

## Generation prompt

Create one finished professional logo symbol for a thoughtful online community forum called Open Circle. This is the final production asset to be used beside a separate typeset wordmark in a website header. Symbol only: absolutely no text, no letters, no tagline, no mockup, no presentation sheet. Design a distinctive geometric open circular conversation mark: a substantial, elegant near-circular burgundy stroke with a deliberate opening at the upper right and a small vivid pink rounded segment bridging the visual conversation, suggesting a welcoming circle and dialogue without a generic chat bubble tail. Balanced optical proportions, clean vector-like edges, flat solid colors, understated modern Scandinavian community brand. Main color deep burgundy #682241; accent pink #EA528E. Strong memorable silhouette legible at 32px. Transparent background, square 1024x1024 canvas, logo centered and occupying about 88 percent of the canvas with consistent tight margins. No gradients, shadows, lighting, texture, bevels, 3D, black background, or other decorative elements. Genuinely polished custom brand mark, suitable for a real social forum.
