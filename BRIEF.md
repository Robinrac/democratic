# Nordic Youth Conference workshops

## Confirmed by Robin

The conference plan includes two workshops. Robin has confirmed that he will personally focus **only on Workshop 2**. Workshop 1 remains background context for how the sessions fit together, not part of Robin's current development scope. All working materials and final titles should be in English. The English titles and descriptions below are the current framing supplied by Robin.

### Workshop 1: Manipulated: Hijacked Message, Changed Decision?

Society focus: explore how manipulated information can affect who takes part in a democratic discussion and, ultimately, whose views influence a decision.

### Workshop 2: Hacked: Who Speaks for You?

Individual focus: examine what happens when someone gains access to a digital platform, participants' information, or the proposals they submit.

It adapts Munia's visual structure with Cybersecurity Academy's basic palette as requested by Robin. Earlier proposals remain in `APP-CONCEPT.md`, `WORKSHOP-2-DESIGN-DRAFT.md` and `RESEARCH-AND-REFINEMENT.md`. The questionnaire remains paused. The prototype is for review and is not publicly deployed.

Robin's more detailed subject list for Workshop 2 is: privacy and digital traces; account security; integrity and impersonation; freedom to participate; and the responsibilities of the individual, platform operator, and consultation organizer. Its central question is: **Can you participate freely if your private activity can be exposed or someone else can speak in your name?**
